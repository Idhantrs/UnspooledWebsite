Get Involved

Breaking the overstimulation cycle takes a whole community. Here's how you can help.

Teens

Take back your time

  • Take our survey and share it with friends
  • Join an upcoming workshop or challenge
  • Try one small change: phone outside the bedroom after 10 PM
  • Talk to someone you trust instead of scrolling when things get hard
Parents & Families

Model healthy habits

  • Set phone-free times together, like dinner or the hour before bed
  • Ask about your teen's online life with curiosity, not judgment
  • Attend one of our family events
  • Share our survey with your teen
Educators & Counselors

Bring Unspooled to your school

  • Host a workshop or presentation for your students
  • Help distribute our survey to reach more students
  • Share expertise on adolescent mental health and habits
  • Connect students with support resources
Organizations & Companies

Partner with us

  • Sponsor events or materials for local students
  • Offer mentorship, data, or technical expertise
  • Provide a venue for community events
  • Help us take our project beyond WW-P
Need support?

You're not alone.

If you or someone you know is struggling with anxiety, depression, or feeling isolated, please talk to a trusted adult or school counselor. Free, confidential help is available 24/7.

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis LifelineCall or text 988
  • Crisis Text LineText HOME to 741741
  • Your school counselorEvery WW-P school has counselors ready to help.
