Who we are

Our Team

We're six students from the West Windsor-Plainsboro community. We started Unspooled because we've been caught in the scroll ourselves.

About Unspooled

Unspooled is a student-led organization based in West Windsor-Plainsboro, New Jersey. We focus on a real problem in our own community, compulsive social media use among teens, and work alongside students, parents, educators, and counselors to do something about it.

Our name says it: we want to help teens "unspool" from the endless thread of short-form content and wind their time back toward the things that matter to them.

How We Work

Everything we do starts with listening to our community. Our approach:

  • Research the problem locally and nationally
  • Survey WW-P students, parents, and educators
  • Pinpoint why teens keep scrolling
  • Design free, accessible solutions
  • Partner with schools and local organizations
  • Launch initiatives and measure their impact

What guides us

Our Values

Empathy, not shame

Social media addiction isn't a character flaw. We meet teens where they are, because we've been there too.

Driven by evidence

Our work is built on national research and our own surveys of WW-P students.

Accessible to everyone

Our solutions are free and built for real teens, not locked behind paywalls or easy to get around.
