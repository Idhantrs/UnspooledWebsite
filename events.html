WW-P Student Social Media Survey

1 to 2 minutesOnline (Google Forms)Open to WW-P students

A quick, anonymous survey about screen time and social media. It also earns you 2 raffle tickets.

Help us gather data about our peers in the West Windsor-Plainsboro Regional School District about screen time and social media use. It's a quick multiple-choice form that only takes 1 to 2 minutes.

Why it matters

Everything Unspooled does starts with listening. Of the first 188 students who responded, 86% said they open an app "for a few minutes" and end up on it longer than planned. Every new response helps us understand why teens keep scrolling, and design workshops, challenges, and resources that actually help.

  • Anonymous: we don't collect your name
  • Responses are only used for the Unspooled project
  • Counts for 2 tickets in our raffle (see above)
