Take Our Survey

Your answers help us understand how social media affects teens in our community, and what would actually help.

Why it matters

Our project is built on real data from real students. Our first survey of 200 WW-P students showed that 83.5% use social media more than they want to. Every new response helps us design solutions that work for our community.

  • Takes just a few minutes
  • Anonymous: we don't collect your name
  • Responses are used only for the Unspooled project

Who should take it?

Middle and high school students in the West Windsor-Plainsboro community. If you're a parent, teacher, or counselor, please share it with the students in your life.
