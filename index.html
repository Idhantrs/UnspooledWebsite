Helping teens cut the thread of endless scrolling.

Unspooled is a student-led team from the West Windsor-Plainsboro community. We're empowering teenagers to break free from compulsive social media habits and take back control of their time, focus, and well-being.

How the trap works

The Overstimulation Cycle

Teens aren't just "on their phones too much." They're caught in a loop where each stage feeds the next.

Overstimulation

Apps built around likes, streaks, and endless feeds deliver quick dopamine spikes.

Real Life Feels Dull

Homework, practice, hobbies, and conversations can't compete with the feed.

Isolation

Less face-to-face time leads to loneliness, anxiety, and a decline in mental health.

Escape to the Phone

To cope with stress and loneliness, teens go back to scrolling, and the cycle restarts.

↻ And the loop starts again.

Our mission

Fight the cycle, not just the screen time.

Phone bans, blocker apps, and gadgets each target one piece of the problem. They're often behind paywalls or easy for students to get around, and they rarely deal with why teens keep scrolling.

Unspooled works with teens, parents, educators, and counselors in our community to tackle the whole cycle, so students can take back control of their digital habits and thrive in 2026 and beyond.

Why We Exist

Teens now average more than 8 hours a day on screens for entertainment, and 83.5% of West Windsor-Plainsboro students we surveyed admit to using social media for more time than they want to. Unspooled exists to empower teenagers in our community to break the compulsive patterns of social media addiction, so they can reclaim control over their digital habits and thrive.
